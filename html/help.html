Site Help & Navigation Guide

This page explains every section of the website so new visitors can quickly understand what each page contains and how to use it.

Home

The Home page is the starting point of the website. It introduces the U.S. Department of Transportation's Office of Aviation Consumer Protection (OACP) and explains its mission. Here you will find an overview of what OACP does, including reviewing consumer complaints, promoting consumer rights, and publishing the Air Travel Consumer Report. Use the navigation bar at the top to visit any other page on the site.

File a Complaint

This page is where air travelers can begin filing a complaint, comment, or compliment about their experience with an airline or travel agency. It explains what information you should have ready before starting (booking details, flight details, and supporting documents) and provides a button to begin the submission process. Read the privacy statement on this page before submitting any information.

Complaint Process

This page describes how the OACP handles complaints after they are submitted. It explains the difference between civil rights complaints (which are individually investigated) and other service complaints (which are reviewed for compliance patterns). It also links to the monthly Air Travel Consumer Report so you can compare complaint records across airlines and travel agencies.

Support

If you experience any technical problems while using this website — such as a broken link, a page that will not load, or a form that does not work — visit the Support page. Fill in the subject and description fields to report the issue. This helps the site maintainers identify and fix problems quickly.

Guest Book

The Guest Book page allows visitors to leave a personal message about the site. Fill in your name, major, gender, hobbies, and a comment, then click "Sign Guest Book" to submit your entry.

Help

You are currently on the Help page. It provides a summary of every page on the website and instructions on how to navigate between them. If you are new to the site, reading this page first will give you a clear picture of where to find the information you need.

About

The About page introduces the student who created this website project. It includes the developer's name, student ID, background in computer science, and a brief personal introduction. Visit this page to learn more about the person behind the site.
